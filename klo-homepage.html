Our philosophy

Keep. Live. Old.
Three words, one refusal to waste.

Fashion moves fast. We didn't want to. KLO was founded on a simple bet: that a garment built well enough, and cared for long enough, becomes worth more with age — not less.

We cut fewer styles, in smaller runs, from fabric chosen to soften and hold rather than fade and fail. What we sell this year, we'll still be repairing in ten.

Made to last

Overbuilt seams, natural fibers, and cuts tested for decades of wear — not one season.

Made to mend

Every garment ships with a repair kit and free mending for as long as you own it.

Made in small runs

We produce to order in limited batches, cutting overproduction and waste at the source.
